- `([]{<>})`: the box `[]` opens and closes inside the tube, then the chest opens inside the tube and the sleeve inside the chest. At that moment the tube, the chest and the sleeve are open together, so the depth is $3$. Every closing symbol matches, and nothing is left open: `OK 3`.
- `(]`: the second symbol tries to close a box, but the innermost open case is a tube: `MISMATCH 2`.
- `[{}`: the chest is opened and closed correctly, but the box opened at position $1$ is never closed. It is the only open case, so it is also the outermost: `UNCLOSED 1`.
