Without tickets ($j = 0$):

- Line 1 has a boat at minute $0$, so the courier boards at once and reaches port 2 at minute $3$. The next boat of line 2 leaves at minute $5$ and reaches port 4 at minute $9$.
- Through port 3 she would leave at minute $2$, arrive at minute $8$, wait for the boat of line 4 at minute $14$ and arrive at minute $16$.
- The direct line 5 only leaves at minute $30$ and arrives at minute $31$.

The best is minute $9$.

With one ticket ($j = 1$) she can charter line 5 at minute $0$ and reach port 4 at minute $1$. The answer is `9 1`.
