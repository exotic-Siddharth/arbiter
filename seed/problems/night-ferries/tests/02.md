Line 1 leaves port 1 at minutes $4, 7, 10, \dots$ and line 2 leaves port 2 at minutes $1, 5, 9, \dots$ Line 3 only sails back to port 1 and never helps.

- $j = 0$: wait for the boat at minute $4$, reach port 2 at minute $6$, wait for the boat at minute $9$ and reach port 3 at minute $14$.
- $j = 1$: charter line 1 at minute $0$ and reach port 2 at minute $2$; the boat at minute $5$ then arrives at minute $10$. Using the ticket on line 2 instead gives minute $6 + 5 = 11$, which is later.
- $j = 2$: charter both lines: $0 + 2 + 5 = 7$.

The answer is `14 10 7`.
