- Choosing $\{2, 4, 8\}$ gives gaps $2$ and $4$, so the closest pair is $2$ m apart. $\{2, 4, 9\}$ also reaches $2$.
- A minimum distance of $3$ is impossible: the hooks at $1$ and $4$ are both within $2$ m of the reserved hook, so the other two lanterns must go at $8$ and $9$, which are only $1$ m apart.
