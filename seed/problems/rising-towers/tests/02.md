With $d = 0$ equal heights may follow each other in a staircase.

- The first three towers all have height $2$, so towers 1, 2, 3 form a staircase and the answers are $1$, $2$, $3$.
- Tower 4 (height $1$) is lower than everything before it and cannot extend any staircase: still $3$.
- Tower 5 (height $2$) extends $2, 2, 2$ to $2, 2, 2, 2$ (towers 1, 2, 3, 5): length $4$.
