- Free: tasks 1 and 5 (codes 3 and 2). Task $5$ goes next.
- Free: only task 1. Task $1$ goes third. This unlocks task 2 (its rules from 1 and 3 are met) and task 4 (rules from 1 and 5 are met).
- Free: task 2 (code 5) and task 4 (code 4). Task $4$ goes before task $2$.
