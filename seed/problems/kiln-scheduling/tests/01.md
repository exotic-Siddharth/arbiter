The kiln starts at hour $2$. All four pieces together need $3 + 2 + 4 + 1 = 10$ hours and would finish at hour $12$, after every pick-up hour, so at most $3$ pieces can be on time.

Fire piece 4 ($1$ hour, due at $4$), then piece 2 ($2$ hours, due at $5$), then piece 3 ($4$ hours, due at $9$). They come out at hours $3$, $5$ and $9$: all on time, and the kiln is free at hour $9$.

The other sets of three do not work: piece 1 needs to be out by hour $6$, and with piece 1 in the set no order brings all three out in time. So the answer is `3 9`.
