Piece 3 needs $8$ hours but is picked up at hour $3$, so it can never be on time.

Pieces 1 and 2 cannot both be on time: whichever goes first, the second comes out at hour $7$, too late for piece 1 (due at $5$) and for piece 2 (due at $6$). So at most $3$ of the remaining pieces 1, 2, 4 and 5 can be on time.

Two sets of three work:

- pieces 1, 4, 5 in that order come out at hours $5$, $7$ and $17$;
- pieces 2, 4, 5 in that order come out at hours $2$, $4$ and $14$.

The second set leaves the kiln free earlier, so the answer is `3 14`.
