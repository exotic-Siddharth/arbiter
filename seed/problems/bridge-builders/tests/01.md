There are $5$ islands, so the archipelago starts with $5$ groups.

- Day 1, bridge $1$-$2$: groups $\{1,2\}, \{3\}, \{4\}, \{5\}$. That is $4$ groups, the largest has $2$ islands, nothing wasted.
- Day 2, bridge $3$-$4$: groups $\{1,2\}, \{3,4\}, \{5\}$: $3$ groups, largest $2$.
- Day 3, bridge $2$-$1$: islands 1 and 2 are already connected, so this bridge is wasted. The groups do not change: `3 2 1`.
- Day 4, bridge $4$-$5$: groups $\{1,2\}, \{3,4,5\}$: $2$ groups, largest $3$.
- Day 5, bridge $1$-$5$ joins the two groups into one group of $5$ islands: `1 5 1`.
