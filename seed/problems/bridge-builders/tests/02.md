- Day 1, bridge $2$-$3$: groups $\{1\}, \{2,3\}, \{4\}$: `3 2 0`.
- Day 2, bridge $1$-$4$: groups $\{1,4\}, \{2,3\}$: `2 2 0`.
- Day 3, bridge $3$-$2$ repeats the bridge of day 1 in the other direction. It is wasted: `2 2 1`.
- Day 4, bridge $4$-$1$ repeats the bridge of day 2 and is wasted too: `2 2 2`.

The two groups are never joined, so the largest group stays at $2$ islands.
