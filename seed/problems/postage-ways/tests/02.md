Design $1$ ($2$ pence) is regular; designs $2$ and $3$ both cost $3$ pence, are different designs, and are commemorative, so each of them can be used at most once.

- $P = 1$: no stamp is that cheap: $0$.
- $P = 3$: design $2$ alone, or design $3$ alone: $2$ ways.
- $P = 4$: two stamps of design $1$: $1$ way.
- $P = 5$: design $1$ with design $2$, or design $1$ with design $3$: $2$ ways.

For $P = 6$ the answer would be $2$: three stamps of design $1$, or designs $2$ and $3$ together (using design $2$ twice is not allowed).
