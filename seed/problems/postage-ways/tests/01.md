All three designs ($1$, $2$ and $5$ pence) are regular, so each can be used any number of times.

- $P = 0$: only the empty collection: $1$.
- $P = 4$: $1+1+1+1$, $1+1+2$ and $2+2$: $3$ ways.
- $P = 5$: $1+1+1+1+1$, $1+1+1+2$, $1+2+2$ and $5$: $4$ ways.
- $P = 7$: with the $5$-pence stamp the remaining $2$ pence are $1+1$ or $2$ ($2$ ways); without it, $7$ is made of ones and twos with zero, one, two or three twos ($4$ ways). In total $6$.
