Four laps of a $400$ m track. Their paces are $96/400$, $90/400$, $102/400$ and $88/400$ seconds per metre.

- The fastest lap is lap $4$ ($88$ s), so it is discarded first.
- Of the remaining laps, the slowest is lap $3$ ($102$ s), which is discarded too.

Laps $1$ and $2$ remain: $D = 800$ m and $T = 96 + 90 = 186$ s. The trimmed pace is $\dfrac{186 / 60}{800 / 1000} = \dfrac{3.1}{0.8} = 3.875$ minutes per kilometre, printed as `3.875000000`.
